THE USEFUL DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Do I need an entirely new brand?

Not necessarily. If your identity has strong foundations, we can refine and strengthen what you already have. A complete rebrand is a custom scope.

Can I start with branding or a website and add the other later?

Absolutely. Each service stands on its own, and we'll document what you've built so adding the next phase is easier. If you add the other service within 12 months and the original scope still fits, we apply the $200 bundle credit toward the next project. Significant rework is quoted separately.

What if I can’t afford the listed starting price?

Tell us what you can spend and what matters most. We can consider a smaller, clearly scoped project if there's a worthwhile result we can deliver within your budget. We're also happy to help you prioritize what to do later; there's no obligation to book a full package.

What if I need more than three pages?

We can quote additional pages or more advanced functionality after understanding the project. The Sprint stays focused so we can deliver it efficiently.

Do you provide domain names and hosting?

We can help you choose and connect a provider, but the domain, hosting, and other third-party subscriptions are separate costs owned by your business.

What do you need from me to begin?

A clear point of contact, your business information, any existing brand assets, the content and imagery you own or have licensed, and timely feedback at agreed checkpoints.

How do payments and timelines work?

For standard packages, choose one payment at kickoff or three project milestones: 50% before kickoff, 25% at design approval, and 25% before launch or final file handoff. We confirm actual amounts, milestones and timing in writing before work begins. Smaller custom projects may use different terms.

Is the three-payment option a loan or monthly subscription?

No. It is milestone-based invoicing tied to the progress of your project, not a fixed monthly plan or financing arrangement. Phalinx does not charge interest for splitting the project this way. We pause work for overdue invoices, and final deliverables are released after the last payment.
